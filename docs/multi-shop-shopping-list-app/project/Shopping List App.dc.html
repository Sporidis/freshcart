Tap a pill, or drag the list sideways (New / Proposal). Tap rows to check them off (several at once settle together). Hold a row for its menu. Turn Liquid Glass on and scroll: rows pass under the tabs. The FAB rolls open; type and press Enter to add fast, or tap + for details. The menu icon opens your lists; the avatars open list settings.
Motion spec · for the Flutter developer
One rule behind every number: the weight of a motion matches the weight of the move. Filtering is light and fast, switching lists is a shared object out of the drawer, side tasks are temporary sheets, and only List settings is a real push. Curves: easeOutCubic is Cubic(.33,1,.68,1) and the iOS curve is Cubic(.32,.72,0,1).
What changed from v1
The list is the home screen again, with the main design's header: menu icon, the small "Basket" label above the list name, and household avatars that open List settings. The lists overview moved into the drawer with its progress bars. The shop tabs are back to plain 36px pills on the paper instead of a 56px floating capsule. That capsule was the "too big, feels off" part: it made a filter look like a navigation bar. The quick-add bar is replaced by the rolling FAB.
1 · Shop switch
New
Highlight
280ms easeOutCubic. One coloured capsule moves left + width to the new pill; unselected pills keep a 1px hairline. The chosen pill then scrolls to the centre (smooth, ~300ms).
Content
220ms easeOutCubic. Rows enter from 28px in the tab direction, opacity 0 → 1, 24ms stagger on the first 6. There's no exit animation, so a fast tapper never waits.
Colour wave
400ms easeOutCubic, no overshoot, from the tapped pill into the header. A new tap cancels the running wave, commits its colour, and starts again.
Swipe
Locks to horizontal after 8px. Content follows 1:1 and fades to 50% at 260px, with 30% resistance at the first or last shop. Commits at 86px or a 450px/s fling, otherwise springs back in 240ms.
Flutter
AnimatedPositioned · ScrollController.animateTo · AnimatedSwitcher keyed by shopId with per-row Interval · CustomPaint clip on an AnimationController, forward(from: 0) · onHorizontalDragUpdate
Proposal · one position drives everything
extra variant
Treat the shop as a continuous value. While you swipe, the highlight, accent and header tint interpolate with your finger, and there's no wave: the tint crossfades over 280ms. The content slide already tells you the shop changed, so the wave isn't needed.
TabController.animation → lerp the highlight Rect + Color.lerp(a, b, t)
2 · Add product (rolling FAB)
Roll open
Width 56 → full row, 380ms on the iOS curve, anchored right. The background goes from accent to surface (glass when on). The input fades in after 160ms, once there's room for it, and focuses at 260ms.
Glyph
The + rotates 45° to an × in 300ms easeOutBack while the field is empty. Typing rotates it back to + and fills it with the accent. So × closes the bar and + opens the details sheet (shop, name, amount, note).
Fast path
Enter adds the item straight to the current shop and keeps the bar open for the next one. Each new row pops in from −6px over 260ms.
Roll closed
280ms Cubic(.4,0,.6,1). The input fades out in 80ms first, so text never gets squashed.
Flutter
AnimatedContainer(width) + AnimatedOpacity(Interval(.4, 1)) · AnimatedRotation(turns: 0.125)
3 · Navigation: three different weights
Drawer
300px wide. Opens in 360ms on the iOS curve, closes in 260ms Cubic(.4,0,.6,1). The scrim goes 0 → 42% in 300ms. The main screen stays put, because the drawer is a place to choose, not a different screen.
Switch list
Shared element, 380ms Cubic(.2,.8,.2,1). The tapped list name flies from the drawer row into the header, scaling 0.62 → 1 (16 → 26pt), while the drawer closes underneath it. The rows rise 12px with a 30ms stagger, starting 80ms in.
Side tasks
Floating sheet, inset 8px, radius 24, used for add product, add shop, new list, join and profile. Opens in 440ms on the iOS curve, closes in 300ms, scrim 0 → 32%. Drag the handle down 110px (or flick it) to dismiss.
List settings
The only real push: 350ms on the iOS curve. The incoming screen slides from 100% to 0, the outgoing one from 0 to −30%.
Flutter
Drawer via a custom AnimatedSlide (the stock Drawer doesn't let you change its curve) · an OverlayEntry for the title flight, measured with RenderBox.localToGlobal · showModalBottomSheet(sheetAnimationStyle) · CupertinoPageRoute
4 · Small touches
Press
Scale 0.97, 140ms easeOutCubic. Small targets (pills, menu, glyph) use 0.90–0.95.
Check-off
The circle fills in the shop colour (scale 0 → 1, 260ms easeOutBack, the one overshoot in the app). The tick fades in after 80ms. Then a 550ms pause, a 220ms collapse, and the row reappears in DONE from −6px.
Progress
4px bar in the accent colour under the title, 420ms easeOutCubic. It counts the item on tap. The drawer's progress bars are ink, since they cover the whole list.
4b · Feedback and the hold menu
Batch settle
Every check restarts a 750ms settle timer, so items you tick in quick succession move together. When it fires, all of them lift (scale 1.015, a soft shadow) and glide to DONE in tap order, 480ms Cubic(.2,.8,.2,1) with a 45ms stagger. The remaining rows close the gaps in 380ms. Tapping a pending row again un-checks it before it moves.
Check pop
The circle scales 1 → 1.2 → 1 (340ms) and a ring in the shop colour expands to 2.2× while fading (480ms). The progress label bumps 1 → 1.16 → 1.
Toast
Ink pill above the FAB, rising 18px in 380ms, held for 3.4s. Every destructive or bulk action gets one with Undo: check-off, clear, delete and move. Finishing a shop says so.
Hold menu
A 450ms hold, cancelled by 8px of movement, plus a light haptic. The row lifts in place: scale .97 → 1.02 → 1 over 340ms. The menu body unrolls with a clip from the top (300ms, starting 90ms in) and the items cascade in 40ms apart. The stepper acts without closing the menu. Move to shop swaps in springy shop chips. Close: fade + scale .97 in 160ms.
Flutter
AnimatedList, or an implicit FLIP using GlobalKeys + RenderBox offsets · Timer for the settle debounce · HapticFeedback.selectionClick() · OverlayEntry anchored to the row's RenderBox for the menu
5 · Liquid Glass
Floating chrome only: the tab strip and its pills, the FAB (tinted glass in the shop colour), the hold menu and sheets. Each gets a 14px blur at 190% saturation, a 30–45% fill and a 1px specular rim along the top edge. With glass on, the list starts under the tab strip, so the rows scroll visibly beneath it. With glass off, the strip is plain paper and the list starts below it.
ClipRRect + BackdropFilter(ImageFilter.blur(sigmaX: 22, sigmaY: 22)). Test the scrolling performance on a low-end Android device.
6 · Reduced motion
Everything becomes a 120ms linear crossfade: the drawer, sheets and settings fade in place, the FAB snaps to its width, the wave becomes a tint fade, and the title stops flying. The check-off pause shortens to 250ms.
