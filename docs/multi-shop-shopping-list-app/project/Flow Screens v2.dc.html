Flow 00

Getting in

Splash, welcome, and the four onboarding steps. The idea holding it together: the app introduces itself by showing the thing it makes — a small stack of shop-coloured rows that builds up as the user answers each question, so by the last step they are looking at their own list rather than a form. Nothing here asks for an account, an email, or a password.

Flow 09

One control, three depths. It rests as a circle, rolls open into a full-width field for the common case (type a name, done), and only escalates to a sheet when the user asks for detail. The split that makes settings work: global settings holds only what is genuinely global (you, appearance, notifications), while shops and household are properties of a list and live in list settings, scoped by a switcher at the top.
