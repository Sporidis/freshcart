Motion spec · for the Flutter developer
One rule behind every number: the weight of a motion matches the weight of the move. Filtering is light and fast, opening a list is a shared object, side tasks are temporary sheets, and only Settings is a real push. Curves: easeOutCubic is Cubic(.33,1,.68,1) and the iOS curve is Cubic(.32,.72,0,1).
1 · Shop switch
New
Highlight
280ms easeOutCubic. One coloured capsule moves left + width to the new pill; the pill then scrolls to centre (smooth, ~300ms). Text flips to on-accent in 200ms.
Content
220ms easeOutCubic. Rows enter from 28px in the tab direction, opacity 0 → 1, 24ms stagger on the first 6, then all together. No exit animation: the old list is simply replaced, so a fast tapper is never waiting.
Colour wave
400ms easeOutCubic, no overshoot, from the tapped pill. A new tap cancels the running wave, commits its colour to the base, and starts again.
Swipe
Locks to horizontal after 8px (and |dx| > 1.2·|dy|). Content follows 1:1 and fades to 50% at 260px. 30% resistance at the first or last shop. Commits at 86px or a 450px/s fling, otherwise springs back in 240ms easeOutCubic.
Flutter
AnimatedPositioned (highlight) · ScrollController.animateTo (centring) · AnimatedSwitcher keyed by shopId, each row wrapped in SlideTransition + FadeTransition with an Interval per index · CustomPaint circle clip on an AnimationController (call forward(from: 0) to restart) · GestureDetector.onHorizontalDragUpdate
Why: the old version had the colour animating for 700ms while the list didn't animate at all, so the two disagreed. Now the content moves first and the colour follows, both finishing within 400ms.
Proposal · one position drives everything
extra variant
Treat the shop as a continuous value (0.0 → 3.0), not as discrete taps. While you swipe, the highlight, the accent colour and the header tint all interpolate with your finger, and there's no wave at all: the tint crossfades over 280ms. The wave and the content slide both tell you the shop changed, so you only need one of them. With this approach a half-swipe shows you exactly where you'll land.
TabController.animation (or PageController.page) → lerp the highlight Rect + Color.lerp(accentA, accentB, t)
2 · Navigation: three different weights
Open list
Shared element, 380ms Cubic(.2,.8,.2,1). The card title flies into the title bar, scaling 0.68 → 1 (19 → 28pt). The overview fades out and the detail fades in over 240ms. Back reverses it.
Side tasks
Floating sheet, inset 8px, radius 24. Opens in 440ms (iOS curve), closes in 300ms Cubic(.4,0,.6,1), scrim 0 → 32% in 240ms. Drag the handle down 110px (or flick it) to dismiss.
Settings
Sideways push, 350ms (iOS curve). The incoming screen slides from 100% to 0, the outgoing one from 0 to −30%.
Flutter
Hero(tag: 'list-title-$id') + PageRouteBuilder with a FadeTransition · showModalBottomSheet(sheetAnimationStyle: AnimationStyle(duration: 440ms, reverseDuration: 300ms)) · CupertinoPageRoute
3 · Small touches
Press
Scale 0.97, 140ms easeOutCubic on press and on release. Small targets (pills, + button) use 0.92–0.96 so the change is still visible.
Check-off
The circle fills in the shop colour (scale 0 → 1, 260ms easeOutBack, the only overshoot in the app: small, local, a reward). The tick fades in over 140ms after an 80ms delay. Then a 550ms pause, the row collapses in 220ms easeOutCubic, and it reappears in DONE from −6px over 260ms.
Progress
3px bar in the accent colour, width animates over 420ms easeOutCubic. It counts the item on tap, not after the pause.
Flutter
AnimatedScale on pointer down/up · AnimatedScale(curve: Curves.easeOutBack) for the fill · SizeTransition to collapse · TweenAnimationBuilder for the progress value
4 · Liquid Glass (floating chrome only)
Only three surfaces: the pill bar, the quick-add bar and sheets. Each gets a 22px blur at 180% saturation, 50% white (dark mode: 50% #2C2C2A), a 1px white rim and an inner top highlight. Everything else stays ink on paper.
What to judge: scroll the list under the pill bar. The coloured check circles show through, which is the whole point of glass and also its risk. Your look is flat and typographic, so the glass reads as an iOS layer on top rather than part of the brand. My suggestion: use it on sheets only, where the content behind is static.
ClipRRect + BackdropFilter(ImageFilter.blur(sigmaX: 22, sigmaY: 22)). The blur is recomputed on every frame over a scrolling list, so test it on a low-end Android device.
5 · Reduced motion
Every transition becomes a 120ms linear crossfade. No travel, scale or overshoot. The wave becomes a tint fade, the highlight is replaced by the selected pill's own fill, the shared title crossfades, and sheets fade in place. The check-off pause shortens to 250ms.
final reduce = MediaQuery.disableAnimationsOf(context); → swap to AppMotion.reduced durations
