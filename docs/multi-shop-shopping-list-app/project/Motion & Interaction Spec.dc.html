01 — Principles

Five rules that make it feel alive

1 · Motion confirms, never decorates

Every animation answers "did that work?" or "where did it go?". If it answers neither, cut it. No looping ambient motion, no decorative parallax.

2 · Overshoot is the personality

Things the user did get a small overshoot (checks, chips, added rows). Things that merely happen (loads, syncs, someone else's edit) stay linear and calm.

3 · Fast in, gentle out

Appearing is 180–260 ms. Disappearing is 120–160 ms. Nothing in the app takes longer than 400 ms except the completion celebration.

4 · Colour follows the shop

Accent motion (checks, tab pills, FAB) tints to the active shop's user-chosen colour via AppTheme.forShop. Structure stays ink.

5 · One spring, one curve, one duration set

Everything in this document is built from the six tokens on the next page. If a screen needs a seventh, it is probably wrong.

04 — Signature moment

Checking an item off

The single most repeated action in the app. It gets the most craft. Four things animate at once, all driven from one 340 ms controller.

LayerFrom → toTiming
Circle fillscale 0 → 1 of the shop-coloured disc, radial from centre340 ms easeOutBack
Checkmark pathstroke draw 0 → 1 (dash offset)200 ms easeOutCubic, 80 ms delay

Then, 600 ms later: the row animates out of its position and re-inserts at the bottom of the list under a "Done · 4" divider — standard 260 ms, driven by AnimatedList or flutter_animate. The delay matters: reordering instantly makes people feel they mis-tapped. Unchecking reverses everything at quick with no overshoot and no reorder delay.

The roll-open capsule

The add control has three depths, and the glyph always states what tapping it will do. Implement as a single AnimatedContainer whose width interpolates while the radius stays pinned at 28 — not a FAB that cross-fades into a different widget.

Two commits, deliberately different. Enter adds the item and leaves the capsule open with the field cleared — six items is six keystroke-bursts, not six round trips. Tapping + opens the detail sheet (name prefilled, shop pills, amount stepper, optional note) for the rare item that needs more. The same sheet, retitled Edit product with a Remove action, is what a row tap opens later.

The capsule is suppressed entirely in multi-select mode and when the list-complete card is showing — it scales to 0 over 180 ms easeInCubic rather than fading, so the corner reads as emptied rather than faded out.

08 — Signature moment

Live edits from other people

The reason the app is shared — so it should be visible, but never startling. Remote changes are calm: no overshoot, no haptic, no sound.

EventTreatment
Someone adds an itemRow slides in from its slot with a 1.5 px left edge in that person's avatar colour; a small avatar chip fades in on the right for 3 s, then fades to the qty text. 300 ms easeOutCubic, no scale pop.
Someone checks an itemSame check animation but at 180 ms with easeOutCubic (no back-curve), and no haptic. Avatar chip appears beside the check for 3 s.
Someone removes an itemRow fades to 30%, holds 400 ms, then collapses its height over 220 ms — the pause prevents "the list jumped".
Person joins the listTheir avatar drops into the header cluster, 340 ms easeOutBack, plus a 2.5 s toast: "Sam joined Weekly Groceries".
PresenceAvatars of people currently viewing sit in the header at full opacity; recent-but-away at 40%. Transition 400 ms linear.
ConflictLast write wins silently, except for quantity: show an inline "Sam set this to 3" caption under the row for 4 s.

Never animate a remote change while the user is dragging, multi-selecting, or has a sheet open — queue it and apply on the next idle frame, so their gesture is never yanked out from under them.

Completing the whole list

The one place the app is allowed to be theatrical — and only when the last unchecked item in the whole list is checked, not per shop tab.

10 — Gestures

Swipe, long-press, drag, pull

Swipe row to delete

Dismissible, direction: endToStart only (left-to-right stays free for a future "move to shop"). Background is AppColors.destructive with a trash icon that scales 0.7 → 1 as the drag passes 30% width. dismissThresholds: {endToStart: 0.4}. At threshold crossing: mediumImpact() and the icon pops to 1.15 then settles. On release below threshold, the row springs back with AppMotion.spring. On dismiss, height collapses over 220 ms and a 4-second undo snackbar appears — the row is only deleted from Firebase when the snackbar expires, so undo is instant and local.

Long-press to multi-select

500 ms press. At 300 ms the row starts scaling to 0.97 as a "keep holding" tell; at fire, mediumImpact() and the row springs back to 1.0. Entering the mode: every row's checkbox slides 8 px right and a selection circle fades in on the left (240 ms, 25 ms stagger down the list), the header cross-fades to a contextual bar ("3 selected · Move · Delete · ✕"), and the FAB scales out. Exiting reverses it. Tapping rows toggles selection with selectionClick() and a 120 ms fill.

Implementation trap: the pointerup ending the long-press still fires a tap on the same row, which would immediately deselect the row the press just selected. Set a flag when the timer fires and have the row's tap handler consume it once. In Flutter, GestureDetector's onLongPress already suppresses the companion onTap, so use those rather than hand-rolling a timer on onTapDown.

Drag to reorder

ReorderableListView within a single shop tab (cross-shop reordering is meaningless — moving between shops is the multi-select Move action instead). Reorder handles appear only in multi-select mode: horizontal swipe owns the row otherwise, and a vertical-vs-horizontal gesture race on the same row is the one ambiguity worth designing out. Pick-up: row lifts to elevation 8, scales to 1.03, opacity 0.95, over 200 ms, with selectionClick(). Other rows part with a 200 ms easeOutCubic gap. Each crossing fires selectionClick(). Drop: settles with AppMotion.spring and lightImpact(). Checked items are not draggable and sit locked below the "Done" divider.

Pull to refresh

RefreshIndicator tinted to the active shop colour (colorScheme.primary). Since Firestore streams are already live, this is a reassurance gesture: it forces a re-fetch and, on completion, shows a 1.2 s "Up to date · just now" caption that fades in under the tab strip. Minimum spinner time 600 ms even if the fetch returns instantly — an instantly-vanishing spinner reads as broken.

16 — Missing flow

Search & suggestions

Search lives behind an icon in the header, not a permanent bar — the list is usually short. Tapping it expands the field across the header (280 ms easeOutCubic, title fades out), the tab strip slides up and out, and results become one flat list across all shops with the shop dot doing the disambiguating. Matching substrings are weight-600 rather than highlighted. Clearing collapses everything back. Search is local-only — no spinner, results filter per keystroke.

Suggestions appear inside the add-product sheet, under the field: a wrap of chips from (1) items bought on this list in the last 30 days, (2) items typed most often, ranked by recency × frequency. Chips stagger in 30 ms apart. Tapping one adds it immediately and the chip flies to the list, leaving the sheet open for the next. With text typed, the chip row becomes matching-history autocomplete instead. Suggestions are per household, not global — no shared product catalogue, nothing to moderate.

Recently bought is the same data on a dedicated screen (drawer → Recently bought): grouped by week, each row with a + that re-adds to the current list with a easeOutBack pop and lightImpact(). This is the "weekly staples" shortcut without building a template feature.
