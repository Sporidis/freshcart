Flow 00

Getting in

Splash, welcome, and the four onboarding steps. The idea holding it together: the app introduces itself by showing the thing it makes — a small stack of shop-coloured rows that builds up as the user answers each question, so by the last step they are looking at their own list rather than a form. Nothing here asks for an account, an email, or a password.

Splash
On screen for as long as the auth check takes, no longer. The mark is a Hero — it flies to the top-left of Welcome rather than cutting.

Flow 09

The add control

One control, three depths. It rests as a circle, rolls open into a full-width field for the common case (type a name, done), and only escalates to a sheet when the user asks for detail. The glyph carries the state: + rotates 45° into × when open and empty, and rotates back to + the moment there is text — so the button always shows what tapping it will do.

1 · Resting
56 px circle, tinted to the active shop. Bottom-right so it never sits under the reading path.
